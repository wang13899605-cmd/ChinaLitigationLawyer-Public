## Matter-specific expansion

Add applicable special laws, judicial interpretations, SPC replies, local or specialist-court rules, arbitration rules, judicial policies, guiding/typical cases, and current official cases. If an old relationship spans legal changes, create separate temporal scenarios. A secondary source may locate an issue but may not alone support a critical conclusion.

## Source record

For each legal proposition record source title, issuing body, document number when available, promulgation/effective dates, current status, provision, official URL or local verified copy, retrieval date, proposition supported, contrary authority, temporal fit, and verifier.
