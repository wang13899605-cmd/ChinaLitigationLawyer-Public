# Current authority baseline

> Retrieval date: 2026-09-13. This is a routing baseline, not a closed list. Recheck current validity and all matter-specific national, specialist, local, concentrated-jurisdiction, and transitional rules before relying on it.

## General civil procedure

1. [National People's Congress Standing Committee decision amending the PRC Civil Procedure Law (2023)](https://www.npc.gov.cn/npc/c2/c30834/202309/t20230901_431419.html). The amended law took effect on 2024-01-01 and materially revised foreign-related jurisdiction, deemed acceptance, parallel proceedings, exclusive jurisdiction and forum non conveniens.
2. [SPC Interpretation on the Application of the PRC Civil Procedure Law, second revision (2022)](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/353651.html). Use with later statutory amendments, judicial interpretations and approvals; do not assume every historical article number remains aligned.
3. [SPC Reply on Certain Questions Concerning the Application of Law to Jurisdiction in Civil Cases, Fa Shi [2025] No. 15](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/485241.html), effective 2025-12-31. It addresses actual connection outside the listed statutory places, specialist-court limits on jurisdiction clauses, determinable regional clauses, arbitration-or-litigation clauses, and liability-insurance jurisdiction.
4. [Revised Provisions on Causes of Action for Civil Cases](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/484231.html), effective 2026-01-01. Use cause of action as a classification and routing input, not a substitute for analyzing relief and the underlying legal relationship.

## Court level and specialist jurisdiction

5. [SPC Notice Adjusting Standards for Intermediate Courts' First-Instance Civil Jurisdiction](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/324151.html), effective 2021-10-01. Its general standards expressly do not govern intellectual-property, maritime or foreign/Hong Kong/Macao/Taiwan cases; verify later and local approvals.
6. [SPC Provisions on Internet Court Jurisdiction, Fa Shi [2025] No. 14](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/478291.html), effective 2025-11-01. It revised concentrated jurisdiction for specified online civil and administrative disputes and preserves matter-specific agreement and appellate rules.

## Foreign-related jurisdiction

7. [SPC Provisions on Jurisdiction over Foreign-Related Civil and Commercial Cases, Fa Shi [2022] No. 18](https://www.court.gov.cn/zixun/xiangqing/379181.html), effective 2023-01-01. Apply alongside the 2023 Civil Procedure Law amendments and any later specialist or local approvals.

## Matter-specific verification prompts

- Has any law, judicial interpretation, reply, official approval, or filing rule changed after the retrieval date?
- Does a specialist court, tribunal, intellectual-property court, maritime court, financial court, internet court, railway court, or other designated court have jurisdiction?
- Has the relevant high people's court published an approved cross-region or concentrated-jurisdiction arrangement?
- Is there a transitional rule based on filing, acceptance, contract, event, or amendment date?
- Does the target court publish a current filing guide or jurisdiction notice, and is it legal authority or only operational guidance?
- Are unofficial database annotations, telephone responses, or counter guidance consistent with current official sources?
